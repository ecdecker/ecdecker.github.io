{{- /* Instrument Serif is the only downloaded family. The serif remains optional. A cold, constrained connection therefore gets a deliberately designed fallback for the whole navigation rather than a late reflow: 1. Prefer locally installed high-contrast display faces. 2. Fall back to a Times-compatible face re-scaled to match Instrument's unusually narrow advances. 3. Measurements across the actual Folio headings put Times-compatible advances at roughly 119% of Instrument's. size-adjust: 84% brings their line lengths back into alignment. The ascent/descent percentages account for that scale (99% / .84 and 31% / .84), preserving Instrument's line box.
