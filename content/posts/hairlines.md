The system has effectively no shadow vocabulary. There is exactly one optional
elevation, and it exists for a single composition: the floating leaf card on a
hero. Everywhere else, separation is a 1px hairline.

## The rule

Cards, panels, inputs, table rows, section dividers, tabs — all use the same
hairline weight. Never a 2px border. Never a double border or inset ring.

> The hairline is the system's voice; thickening it is incorrect.

## What it costs

Hairlines demand more from spacing. A shadow implies depth even when elements
are crowded; a hairline does not. Once you commit to hairlines, whitespace stops
being decoration and becomes the load-bearing material.

That is the trade the system makes deliberately. Generous whitespace over visual
chrome, every time.

## What it buys

One weight, applied everywhere, means a page can gain sections without gaining
visual noise. Nothing competes. The headline stays the loudest thing on the
page, which is the entire point of an editorial layout.
